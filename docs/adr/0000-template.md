# ADR-0000: Template — one line naming the decision, not the topic

- Status: proposed
- Date: YYYY-MM-DD
- Source: the clause in REQUIREMENTS.md or docs/SPEC.md this record serves

## Context

What forces the choice: the constraint, the failure mode being avoided, what
was true before. Enough that somebody who was not here can disagree with it
on the merits. No solution yet.

## Decision

What was decided, in the present tense and specifically enough to check a
diff against. Name the boundaries, the thresholds and the words that must
appear or must not.

## Consequences

What this costs, what it makes harder, and what becomes possible. Honest
about the downside; a record with no cost listed was not a decision.

## Enforcement

The test, policy or build setting that makes this real, by name. If nothing
checks it yet, say so — that is a gap a reviewer is entitled to raise.
